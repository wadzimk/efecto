About EFECTO

🔬 Not an expert. An observer.

This project started as personal notes — a habit of writing down what I noticed after each session. Strain name, effects, location, context. After 130+ documented strains across multiple countries, the patterns became too interesting to keep private.

The data here represents 25 of the most interesting strains from that archive. One observer, one consistent methodology, confirmed and challenged by friends during shared sessions.

Why effects?

Strain names and THC percentages tell you almost nothing about what an experience will actually feel like. Effects are what matters. This atlas is built around that idea.

The future

The long-term goal is a community app where everyone can log effects on a 0–5 scale for any strain they try. Over time, the data becomes genuinely useful: looking for something to help you sleep? Filter by sleepy.
